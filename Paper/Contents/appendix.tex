\section{Probabilistic Verification of Reed--Solomon Codewords}
\label{app:rs-verify}

Let $\mathbb{F}$ be a finite field, let
$\vect{m}=(a_0,\ldots,a_{k-1}) \in \mathbb{F}^k$, and let
$\vect{c}=(c_0,\ldots,c_{n-1}) \in \mathbb{F}^n$ for $n \ge k$.
Let $S=\{w^0,w^1,\ldots,w^{n-1}\}\subseteq\mathbb{F}$ be the evaluation
domain.  Define the message polynomial
$f(X)=\sum_{i=0}^{k-1}a_iX^i$ and let $g(X)$ be the unique polynomial of
degree less than $n$ satisfying $g(w^i)=c_i$ for every $i$.
The goal is to test whether $f(X)\equiv g(X)$, i.e., whether $\vect{c}$ is
the Reed--Solomon encoding of $\vect{m}$.

The verifier samples $z\sample\mathbb{F}\setminus S$, computes
$v_1=f(z)$ by Horner evaluation, and computes $v_2=g(z)$ by barycentric
interpolation:
\[
  v_2 =
    \frac{\sum_{i=0}^{n-1} \frac{\lambda_i}{z-w^i}c_i}
         {\sum_{i=0}^{n-1} \frac{\lambda_i}{z-w^i}},
\]
where $\lambda_i$ are the barycentric weights for $S$.
The verifier accepts iff $v_1=v_2$.

If $f(X)\not\equiv g(X)$, then $h(X)=f(X)-g(X)$ is a nonzero polynomial of
degree less than $n$.
By Schwartz--Zippel,
\[
  \Pr_{z\sample\mathbb{F}}[f(z)=g(z)] \le \frac{n-1}{|\mathbb{F}|},
\]
which is negligible when $|\mathbb{F}|$ is sufficiently large.
